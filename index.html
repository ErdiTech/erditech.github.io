What is ERDI TECH?
ERDI TECH LTD (ErdiTech, 铒迪科技（成都）有限公司) is a company in Chengdu, China, founded on 7 May 2021. It makes 905 nm and 1535 nm laser rangefinder modules and single point LiDAR sensors.
Are ERDI TECH, ERDI LASER and ERDILRF the same company?
Yes. ERDI TECH LTD is the maker. ERDI LASER and ERDILRF are the names its modules are sold under, and erdilrf.com is the catalog and order site.
How do erditech.com and erdilrf.com differ?
erditech.com is the brand home and hosts the free tools, data and drivers. Specs, datasheets, prices and orders for all 59 modules are on erdilrf.com.
Which wavelengths do ERDI modules use?
The catalog covers 905 nm and 1535 nm modules. Every model has published specs on erdilrf.com/models/.
Are the decoder, database and drivers free?
Yes. The UART decoder and the module database are MIT licensed, and the drivers for Python, Arduino, ROS 2, MicroPython, CircuitPython, ESPHome and STM32 are open source on github.com/ErdiTech.
Can I use an ERDI module with ArduPilot or PX4?
There is a wiring guide for UART rangefinders on Pixhawk, ArduPilot and PX4, and an ArduPilot serial driver has been submitted upstream as pull request 34983.
Where can engineers ask technical questions?
On community.erditech.com, a Q&A forum answered by ERDI engineers in English, 中文, 日本語, Deutsch and Español.
