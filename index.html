Free · MIT

LiDAR / laser rangefinder UART decoder

Paste a hex dump or connect a module over WebSerial. It detects the protocol and baud rate, converts frames to metres and flags checksum errors, for Benewake, LightWare, Chengdu JRT, Nooploop, DFRobot and ERDI modules.
